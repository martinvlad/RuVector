The helper uses only Python's standard library. It verifies exact sizes/SHA256,
rejects path/symlink/hash mismatches, creates only verified regular files and
reuses exact existing assets. Existing mismatches are never overwritten. All
local cache files and existing targets are checked before any write/network.
Downloads stream to temporary files and are admitted only after exact identity
verification. A failed later download may leave earlier complete verified files;
retrying reuses them. Receipt paths must be new. No inference or addon executes.
