# Epoch12: fixed FP32 typed reader and TRAIN-only variant fidelity

Epoch11's INT8 reader predicted entailment for every new DEV example under all three selectors. Epoch12 fixes the primary reader to the published FP32 ONNX export of the same immutable converter repository revision. It does not choose a precision from the results. One paired FP32/INT8 diagnostic runs on each of six frozen TRAIN inputs from two already exposed documents; no labels are loaded by the candidate and no parameters are fitted. DEV and conditional confirmation use FP32 only.

The pretrained reader remains DeBERTa-v3-large trained on MNLI, FEVER-NLI, ANLI, LingNLI and WANLI, with 24 layers and hidden size1024. E0/Neutral1/C2 are retained and normalized together at temperature1. Binary Yes=P(Entailment), No=P(Contradiction)+P(Neutral), with No on an exact half tie. Higher precision is a hypothesis about numerical fidelity, not an established legal-accuracy improvement.

The FP32 ONNX is exactly1741989392 bytes with SHA256 `9fbf57959bd9143720289cd1eab1aaaa00fc99459fd25ffeff8eba1ccf955d2b`. Both variants use the exact epoch11 tokenizer/configuration at revision `a70e12f8244efae07cf6fdfa935df30e3ac4a060`. The upstream source card declares MIT, while mixed training-data licenses, converter license and exact upstream weight-commit gaps remain disclosed. Co-located FP32/INT8 exports do not prove their original-source fidelity or a bit-exact conversion derivation; differences alone do not establish quantization causality. No weights are published.

Actual native RuVector evidence, BGE embeddings, source partitioning, up-to40 ranked anchors, paragraph neighbors, source-order packing and complete hypotheses follow the exact frozen epoch6 policy. All three FP32 retrieval arms share that context policy, no padding/truncation and independent512-token guards. Native104 reads the identical primary packed excerpt, not its original submitted full-input champion. Numerical/source-position retention is diagnostic, never an evidence-sufficiency certificate.

The parent alone executes one phase1 process including all initialization, TRAIN6, INT8 release, DEV24 and cleanup under600seconds/CPU2. The optional second process is frozen FP32 confirmation48 only after accuracy/proper-score/statistical/runtime gates. It initializes no INT8 session. There is no fitting, calibration, threshold search, fallback, retry or unmetered forward. A partial TRAIN failure retires the attempted identity and blocks DEV. TRAIN calls enforce exact frozen input hashes and permit each ID once; completion requires all six before releasing INT8 and opening DEV.

The paired diagnostic saves exact identical token IDs/masks/types, full three-way logits/probabilities and scalar differences including centered-logit differences, Jensen-Shannon divergence and decision agreement. It measures the two published variants on exposed TRAIN inputs, not fresh benchmark generalization. The public pilot is internal ContractNLI; it establishes no current JevBench300, full LegalBench or ClaimBench rank. Official SOTA needs a matching publisher result and eligibility. The submitted champion remains untouched unless all independent gates pass.
